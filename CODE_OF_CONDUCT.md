# Code of Conduct

Participation in `pisama-verifier-gym` should be professional, respectful, and
focused on making verifier evaluation more auditable.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and constructive in technical discussion.
- Treat disagreement as part of the review process.
- Disclose conflicts, data provenance concerns, and known limitations clearly.
- Respect privacy and licensing boundaries around datasets and traces.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Submitting private, confidential, or non-redistributable data as public test
  artifacts.
- Misrepresenting verifier metrics, provenance, or label lineage.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to conduct@pisama.ai. Security-sensitive reports should
go to security@pisama.ai instead.
